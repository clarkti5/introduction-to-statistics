# An Introduction to Statistics
This repository contains free materials for an introductory statistics course with applications implemented in Python. Elements of [OpenIntro Statistics by Diez, Çetinkaya-Rundel, and Barr](https://www.openintro.org/book/os/) were adapted into Colab Notebooks for a more hands-on overview of the concepts.

## Student Learning Outcomes

The content here is intended to assist students obtain proficiency in the following.

**Understanding Data**

- Determine when conclusions may be generalized from sample to population as well as when it is appropriate to draw *causal* conclusions by identifying representative sampling techniques and differentiating between observational and experimental studies.
- Interpret common representations of data, including data matrices and data visualizations, and differentiate between categorical and numerical variables in a dataset.
- Use concepts of center, shape, and spread to describe datasets and distributions, along with concepts of direction, shape, and strength to describe relationships between variables. 
- Construct point estimates of statistical parameters, as well as compute and interpret summary statistics, such as the mean, median, standard deviation, and interquartile range.

**Probability & Distributions**

- Demonstrate facility in applying the laws of elementary probability.
- Compute and interpret expectation, variance, and standard deviation of discrete random variables. 
- Recognize the binomial, geometric, normal, $t$, chi-square, and $F$-distributions and use them to compute probabilities and draw conclusions from data.

**Statistical Inference**

- Perform statistical inference through confidence intervals and hypothesis testing for proportions and differences of proportions.
- Perform statistical inference through confidence intervals and hypothesis testing for means and differences of means.
- Advance their applications of statistical inference to chi-square tests for independence and goodness-of-fit, as well as ANOVA.

**Linear Regression**

- Construct and interpret regression lines, including statistical inference on linear model parameters.

**Applications**

- Use technology or computer software (e.g., Python) to solve a variety of statistical problems using real-world data.
